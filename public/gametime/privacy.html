Effective November 12, 2026

Game Time is a soccer lineup and game management app published by C7 Consulting and IT Services, LLC, Lehi, Utah ("we", "us").

The short version

Game Time does not collect your data. Information is stored on your device unless you choose an export or an external calendar link. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your information, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

What the app stores on your device

Information you type in: team names, player names and jersey numbers, positions, game results and notes, schedules and locations, and app settings. Coaches may enter names of players who are children. This information is saved only in the app's storage on your phone. We never receive it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or sell this information. User-requested exports and external links can transmit it to the service you choose.
  • We do not use ads, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Things you choose to do

  • Backup files, reports, lineups and calendar files. When you choose a backup, report, lineup or calendar-file export, the app saves a file or opens your phone’s share menu. The file goes only where you send it (for example Files, Google Drive or iCloud Drive, email, or a messaging app). That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Reminders. If you allow notifications, the app schedules game reminders on your phone. They are created and shown on your device.
  • Device backups. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's files, depending on your device settings. That is controlled by Apple or Google and your settings.

Optional external services

Choosing Open in Google Calendar sends the event title (including team, opponent and home/away status), date, time, location, event label, match number and time zone to Google in the link. This happens only when you choose that option; Google’s privacy policy applies. The installed store app bundles fonts. The web version may load fonts from Google, which receives network information such as your IP address.

Children

Game Time is made for coaches and other adults. It is not directed to children. Because player information stays on your device and is never sent to us, we do not hold any child's personal information.

Your control

You can edit or remove any information in the app at any time. About, privacy and data, then Delete all my data removes everything the app stores. Deleting the app also removes it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and the version in the app.
