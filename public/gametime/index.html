Game Time

Soccer lineups, subs and playing time for coaches.

Game TimeSupportPrivacy

Game Time helps soccer coaches plan lineups, make subs fast, and keep playing time even for every player. It works offline on the sideline.

What it does

  • Pick a formation and set your starting lineup on a real field.
  • Build a sub plan from who is available and how long each player has played.
  • Run the game with a match clock, sub windows, goals, cards and live minutes.
  • Save games, fix mistakes later, and share lineups or PDF reports.
  • Manage your season schedule, roster, jersey numbers, positions and guest players.
