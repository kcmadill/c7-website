Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Game Time has no account and does not send your information to us.

How do I back up my data?

Open the menu, tap Backup and restore, then Save backup file. Your phone's share menu opens so you can save the file to Files, Google Drive or iCloud Drive. Do this after every game.

How do I move to a new phone?

Save a backup file on the old phone. On the new phone, open Game Time and choose I have a backup file on the welcome screen, or use Backup and restore and pick the file.

How do I delete everything?

Open the menu, tap About, privacy and data, then Delete all my data (tap twice to confirm). Deleting the app also removes it.

The game reminders do not show up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Game Time in your phone's settings. Reminders arrive one hour before kickoff, or at 8 am on game day if no kickoff time is set.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything works offline.
